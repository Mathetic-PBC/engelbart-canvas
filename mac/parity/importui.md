Area: `src/renderer/import/**` (ImportFlow, ImportStatus, NeedPopup, ProviderPopup, AgentSetup, ObsidianPicker,
import-api, logos, import-css/round3-css/round4-css, standalone), `src/renderer/screens/ConnectLibrary.jsx`,
`src/renderer/ui/ConnectDock.jsx`, `src/renderer/ui/connect-open.js`, `src/renderer/model/connect.js`, the parts of
`App.jsx` that place them, and the helpers of `model/onboarding.js` / `model/tools.js` these screens call
(keychainStep, afterAllow, profilesOf, runKeychain, agentChoice, cliStage, rowOf), ported locally (Screens and Tools own
the originals; see API.md "Needs"). Swift: `mac/Packages/ImportUI` (module `EngelbartImportUI`); paths in the Swift
column are relative to `Sources/EngelbartImportUI/`.

Status: `done` = asserted by an XCTest that passed `mac/scripts/cloud-test.sh ImportUI`; `partial: …` says what is not;
`n/a: …` Electron-only mechanics. View rows are "drawn offscreen" (laid out and drawn into a bitmap, pixels sampled) and
their behaviour driven through the screen's model; nobody has looked at them in a running app.
