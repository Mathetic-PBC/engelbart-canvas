Round 2: the bugs you found

White strip when zoomed out

There were three causes:

  • Pages that hadn't been drawn yet showed as white during and just after a pinch. Every page within 12 of the view now gets a small low-resolution drawing in the background. Pages that have been drawn keep a small copy after their full drawing is freed, and that copy shows until the full drawing comes back.
  • Zooming out near the top of a paper left an empty band above page 1 until the pinch ended, then the paper jumped up. The paper's top now stays at the pane's top during the pinch.
  • Some white is real. Pages 3 and 5 of etherphone-75pp are blank in the PDF itself, and with no page edges a blank page reads as a gap.

Known limit: a page more than 12 pages from where you've been has no small drawing yet. If you pinch out far from anywhere you've read, a few pages can be white for about a second.

Replaced: round 2 moved notes in over the text when zoomed in. Since round 4 they hide instead (see the top).
